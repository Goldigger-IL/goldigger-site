## 1. Which markets are largest in NIS and most fragmented, with the weakest existing platforms? (per-market fact base)

### Takeaway
By money, the biggest fragmented consumer-service pools are used-car trading (1.29M ownership transfers in 2025), residential rental (about 892K rented dwellings, 81% let by private individuals), private health spending (about a third of NIS 146B), long-term care (NIS 21.1B of Bituach Leumi spending, about 392K beneficiaries), renovations (NIS 15-20B, a third undeclared), garages (5,200 licensed garages) and events (NIS 13.5B in 2016). In nearly all of them the "platform" is a lead-generation or review directory (Yad2, Midrag, Easy, Mit4Mit) that never touches the payment; I found no Israeli transactional platform of scale in renovations, garages, gold buying, movers, tutoring, gravestones or home-care staffing.

**F. Gold, jewellery, precious-metal buying and pawn-type trade**
- [E] Gold rose more than 60% in January-December 2025 and more than 100% in two years, to about $4,200 an ounce; consumer sites describe a growing market of households selling coins, bars and old jewellery. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/economy/2498793/); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/mutualfunds/news/article/20024702)
- [E] Scrap price quotes vary widely over time: one buyer guide showed 14K at NIS 87.3 per gram (older) and about NIS 237 per gram (recent); price is spot multiplied by purity, and each buyer sets its own margin with no published benchmark. — [Financa](https://financa.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%94%D7%91-14-%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%98-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%99/)
- [V] Trust problems: jewellery shops buying old gold usually pay with a store voucher, not cash; a documented case of jewellery sold as 14K that proved to be 9K; the Standards Institution publishes guidance (585 = 14K). — [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/rkkve0p9k); [Standards Institution of Israel](https://www.sii.org.il/lobby/information/jewelry/); [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/567071)
- [V] Regulation: the anti-money-laundering regime was extended to precious-stone dealers (2014-2015); the order does **not** apply to metals such as gold or silver; thresholds NIS 75,000 for cash purchases in jewellery shops and NIS 50,000 between dealers; "a market of more than 2,000 dealers" with about 60 inspections (Knesset committee, c. 2017). — [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/40533); [Knesset committee protocol](https://production.oknesset.org/pipelines/data/committees/download_document_committee_session/files/23/4/9/493573.DOC)
- [V] Pawn-type lending falls under the Fair Credit Law (2017), which caps interest at the Bank of Israel rate plus 15% for shekel loans. — [Protocol.co.il](https://protocol.co.il/fair-credit/)

**G. Elderly home care and nursing (סיעוד)**
- [V] About 392,000 people were entitled to the Bituach Leumi long-term-care benefit at end-2025, up from about 180,000 in 2018; share of the elderly rose from about 16% to about 30%; spending rose from about NIS 7B to about NIS 21.1B; the fund's depletion year moved forward by 6.3 years. Income test (2025): above about NIS 13,300 a month, half benefit; above about NIS 20,000, none. — [State Comptroller, Population Aging report 2026](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2026/Population-Aging/2026-Population-Aging-102.pdf)
- [E] About 70,000 foreign caregivers worked in Israel as of 2025, mostly from East Asia and Eastern Europe. *(attribution approximate)* — [Walla](https://news.walla.co.il/item/3739163); [State Comptroller, Non-Israeli Workers 2026](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2026/Infrastructure-PartB/2026-Infrastructure-PartB-Non-Israeli-Workers-Taktzir.pdf)

Benefit can be taken partly in cash. Foreign recruiters may charge the worker up to NIS 4,389.61 (2026). — [Kol Zchut: employing a foreign caregiver](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93_%D7%96%D7%A8_%D7%91%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93); [Kol Zchut: cash benefit](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA_%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%AA_%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93_%D7%91%D7%9B%D7%A1%D7%A3); [Kol Zchut: fee cap](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%92%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA_%D7%A1%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9D_%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A_%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA%D7%9F_%D7%9C%D7%92%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93_%D7%96%D7%A8)

**L. Private medicine and paramedical services**
- [V] National health expenditure 2024: NIS 146B (7.3% of GDP); private financing (households, private insurance, donations) 33.6%; households alone finance almost a fifth, mainly direct payments to doctors, dentists, medicines and equipment. — [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/economy/israel/article-1251662); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/20023763)

- [E] Private therapy costs NIS 400-700 a session, i.e. NIS 20.8-36.4K a year weekly; via an HMO-arrangement independent therapist 52 sessions cost about NIS 8.4-8.8K. — [N12](https://www.mako.co.il/news-money/2026_q3/Article-9c557d9643daf91027.htm)

## 2. Where does Yad2 dominate lead generation without handling the transaction? Yad2 revenue, ownership, pricing behaviour

### Takeaway
Yad2 is a roughly EUR 73-83M-revenue, high-margin classifieds monopoly now owned by Apax (bought from KKR for about $950M in a deal signed in December 2025), earning 56% of revenue from real estate and 27% from cars, mostly from brokers and dealers rather than consumers. ## 3. Which Israeli marketplaces succeeded and why, and which failed or stalled

### Takeaway
Local winners share high frequency and payment capture (Wolt at 27-30% commission with about half of a roughly NIS 5B food-delivery market; Gett sold to Pango for $175M; BuyMe valued at about NIS 1B) or a listings monopoly (Yad2). Failures cluster in low-frequency or thin-margin local plays and in general e-commerce marketplaces that could not reach scale in a 10-million-person market (Azrieli.com, Shufersal marketplace, Groo/Baligam, Avo, Zeek, Engie, Missbeez), and directories without payment are stagnating (Zap revenue down about 25% since 2021).

### Cited Findings

- [V] Gett: Pango agreed in May 2024 to buy it for $175M; Gett's Israeli activity was profitable with EBITDA of NIS 40-50M. — [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/hkt7lwqzc); [Taxi Point](https://www.taxi-point.co.uk/post/taxi-app-gett-acquired-by-pango-in-175-million-purchase)

Localize/Madlan closed its US arm in 2024. — [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3772275,00.html); [Startupim](https://startupim.com/company/missbeez/raw); [The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/national/2024/08/20/localize-leaving-united-states-behind/)

- [I] Success pattern: frequency of weekly or more (food, taxis, parking, gift cards) lets the platform own payment and makes leakage pointless. Failure pattern: annual-or-rarer purchases (car repair, beauty-at-home, weddings) with high customer-acquisition cost per transaction and easy disintermediation after the first contact.
- [I] In low-frequency Israeli verticals the survivors are advertising/lead models (Yad2, Midrag, Mit4Mit, Easy), which implies a new entrant needs either a managed, full-stack offer (own the job, price and guarantee) or a financial product (guarantee, escrow, financing) to justify routing payment.

## 4. Role of WhatsApp/Facebook groups and the cash/undeclared economy; effect of the Israel Invoices reform and cash limits

### Takeaway
The undeclared share is material exactly where marketplaces are missing (about a third of renovation turnover per D&B; tutoring partly in cash), and the 2024-2026 measures raise the cost of staying off the books mainly for business-to-business invoices of NIS 15,000-25,000 and above, not for a typical NIS 500-5,000 household job. I found no survey quantifying WhatsApp/Facebook-group referrals.

### Cited Findings
- [E] About one third of the roughly NIS 15B renovation market is unreported (D&B; older). — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/355346)
- [V] Israel Invoices (חשבוניות ישראל): an allocation number from the Tax Authority is required for input-VAT deduction on invoices above NIS 25,000 (2024), NIS 20,000 from 1 Jan 2025 (pre-VAT), and NIS 15,000 in 2026 (one source); the model was made permanent and accelerated. — [Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/finance/1427/154523/); [Finbot](https://finbot.helpjuice.com/he_IL/חשבונית-ישראל-עדכון-2025-שלב-ב); [Tax Authority circular 3/2026 via Alliott](https://alliott.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/חוזר-3-2026-תיקוני-חקיקה-לצמצום-תופעת-ההון-השחור.pdf)
- [V] Circular 3/2026 (9 Feb 2026) lists four tools from the 2025 economic-plan law: denial of expense deductions (and input VAT) where the cash law was breached and a fine imposed; wider detailed VAT reporting; permanent Israel Invoices; digitised invoices with Palestinian dealers. Effective 1 Aug 2025, partly 1 Jan 2026. — [Alliott](https://alliott.co.il/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/חוזר-3-2026-תיקוני-חקיקה-לצמצום-תופעת-ההון-השחור.pdf); [Efraty](https://www.efraty.com/?p=5441)
- [E] One opinion piece attributes about NIS 12B of the NIS 40B by which 2025 tax collection beat forecast to Israel Invoices (single, non-official source). — [Ice opinion](https://www.ice.co.il/opinions/news/article/1108530)
- [V] Cash law: the ceiling for a cash payment to a business is NIS 6,000 per transaction (lowered from NIS 11,000; sources seen conflict on the date, the reduction is generally dated 1 Aug 2022); NIS 15,000 between private persons for some cases. The 2026 Arrangements bill extends the limit to cashing cheques, and revives a proposal to restrict holding more than NIS 200,000 in cash (debated in the Knesset Constitution Committee, Feb-Mar 2026). — [Bhol](https://www.bhol.co.il/news/895296); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1284853); [Capitax](https://www.capitax.co.il/content/2/print/3485); [Tax Authority circular 06/2026](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-06-2026/he/IncomeTax_inst-06-2026.pdf)
- [V] Gold/jewellery: AML cash thresholds are much higher (NIS 75,000 in jewellery shops) and the order excludes gold and silver. — [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/40533)

### Inferences
- [I] The reform does not touch the core household-services leak: a consumer cannot deduct VAT, so a plumber's NIS 800 cash job or a NIS 5,900 instalment stays invisible. It does bite on renovation contractors buying materials or subcontracting above NIS 15,000-20,000 and on business customers, pushing mid-size contractors toward full invoicing; that modestly reduces the price gap between "with invoice" and "without" and so reduces a platform's handicap.
- [I] A platform that forces card payment adds 18% VAT plus income tax visibility for the supplier; unless it gives something in return (guaranteed payment, financing for the customer, insurance, steady demand), suppliers will take the lead and close off-platform. This is the main structural blocker in renovations, tutoring, movers and gold buying.
- [I] WhatsApp neighbourhood/parent groups and Facebook groups function as free, trusted referral networks; they solve discovery but not price transparency, quality guarantee or recourse, which is where a platform must differentiate (inference; no data found).

### Gaps
- No quantitative evidence on WhatsApp/Facebook groups as a channel for finding tradespeople (one search returned nothing relevant).
- No official 2024-2026 estimate of Israel's shadow economy by sector, and no evaluation of whether Israel Invoices changed behaviour in consumer services.
- Exact legal dates and thresholds of the cash law should be re-checked against the statute; summaries conflicted.

## 5. Gurley's factors by market

### Takeaway
No single Israeli market scores well on all of Gurley's factors: the large, fragmented, low-trust markets (renovations, garages, used cars, rentals) are low-frequency and leak payments, while the high-frequency ones (tutoring, childcare, home care) are relationship-based and disintermediate after the first match. ### Inferences
- [I] Israeli venture capital overwhelmingly funds global B2B; a marketplace aimed only at Israel should plan on angels, strategic partners (insurers, banks, credit-card companies, importers: note Isracard in WeCheck, The Phoenix in BuyMe, Carasso in Freesbe, Mimun Yashir in CarWiz) or bootstrapping, with roughly NIS 3-10M to reach proof in one vertical and one metro area.
- [I] The repeated pattern of exits to strategic owners suggests the realistic outcome for a local vertical marketplace is acquisition by a financial or automotive group at tens to low hundreds of millions of shekels, not a venture-scale outcome, unless the model exports.
- [I] Because Yad2, Google and Meta control demand, paid acquisition for a once-in-years purchase is expensive per transaction; the cheaper routes are supply-side tools (give tradespeople/garages software and payments, then aggregate demand) or partnerships with bodies that already own the customer (insurers, HMOs, municipalities, employers' benefit clubs).
